# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small football tournament and league organisers working from school, academy, community, club-network and sports-facility settings. They need a dependable official record for fixtures, submitted results, reviewed outcomes, standings and reports without adopting a hosted tournament platform.

## Product Purpose

Football Competition Results & Standings Manager is a manually prepared, customer-owned Google Sheets and Google Forms workflow. It helps organisers create or enter fixtures, collect match results, approve official outcomes and keep standings current. The public site must help an eligible organiser decide whether the offer fits, understand the delivery requirement and visit Selar with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable and practical. It should feel like an organiser's working record that is already under control: clear enough for an infrequent buyer, precise enough for a person responsible for official results.

## Anti-references

- Generic sports SaaS marketing with football decoration instead of product proof.
- AI-generated dashboards, mockups, stock football photography or made-up product screens.
- Repeated all-caps kickers, equal card grids, divider-heavy sections and decorative side stripes.
- Dense legal text that hides the privacy model.
- Price tactics that make a prepared ownership-transfer service look like a generic download.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the organiser's official record, then show the real workflow that makes it trustworthy.
- Let authentic workbook and Form screens carry the evidence.
- Explain delivery, account eligibility and customer ownership plainly before the buyer reaches checkout.
- Use fewer, stronger sections. Every heading and surface must earn its place.
- Treat privacy as a factual explanation of customer control, not a marketing claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA as the working target for the static site. Preserve semantic landmarks, logical headings, visible keyboard focus, meaningful link text, sufficient contrast, readable line lengths and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish links or actions.
