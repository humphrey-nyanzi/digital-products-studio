# Purpose

This backlog records product opportunities discovered through Studio research.

It is not an approval document. Ideas listed here remain candidates until they pass the Studio product lifecycle: research, requirements, planning, design, development, QA, packaging and release approval.

The current strategic priority is to develop sports-related products first. Non-sports systems may remain in research or internal testing until they are ready to be commoditised.

# Current Product Priority

## Product 001: Football Competition Results & Standings Manager

**Category:** Sports Competitions

**Sport Focus:** Football / soccer first

**Status:** Working prototype v0.1.0, final clean-copy QA pending


**Product Folder:** `products/product-001-competition-results-standings-manager/`

**Implementation Note:** The live July workbook has a tested end-to-end lifecycle. Current product status and release work are recorded in `products/product-001-competition-results-standings-manager/docs/prototype_status.md` and `products/product-001-competition-results-standings-manager/docs/release_plan.md`.
**Primary Direction:** A practical competition operations system for organisers who need reliable fixture, result, standings and reporting workflows without adopting a heavy platform.

**Initial User Candidates:**

* Small league organiser
* School tournament organiser
* Academy or club competition admin
* Local federation or amateur league admin

**Likely Technology:**

* Google Sheets
* Google Forms
* Microsoft Excel export where practical
* Apps Script only where it clearly improves reliability or usability
* Markdown documentation

**Reason For Priority:**

This idea is strongly aligned with the Studio's sports-first direction, has clear operational pain, supports modular product development and creates a natural path from template product to custom implementation.

# Reusable Product Patterns

The following patterns may support multiple products:

* Google Forms to Google Sheets intake
* Protected formula sheets
* Visible user sheets plus hidden system sheets
* Setup guide, user manual, release notes and known limitations
* Fictional demo data
* Audit/change log
* Versioned release package
* Template product plus custom implementation service
* Folder structure for specs, reports, exports, reference, handoffs and archive

---

# Business Notes

Initial business research suggests the Studio should avoid launching as a generic template shop.

The stronger model is:

1. Validate one narrow operational pain.
2. Build a reliable template product with documentation and example data.
3. Offer a premium bundle where the extra value is clear.
4. Use successful products as entry points for paid custom implementation.

For Product 001, the most promising commercial path is a football / soccer competition results and standings system that can be sold as a template and customised for schools, leagues, academies, clubs and local competition organisers.
