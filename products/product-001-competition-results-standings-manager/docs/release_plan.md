## Packaging Boundary

The v1.0 deliverable is a reusable workbook, linked Form, Apps Script automation and buyer guide. It does not include ongoing competition administration, custom formula rebuilding, player registration, scorer tracking, disciplinary administration or unlimited support.

## Current Blocker

Core product functionality, football logic, ordinary-user operation, controlled ownership transfer, isolation and protections are substantially verified. The standard Cloud project, product application identity, support route, support-owned public manual and verified branding are established. The remaining external release blocker is Google's data-access review. The remaining Studio gates are pricing, payment, packaging, support boundaries, buyer testing and the Director release decision.

Google states that the first Trust and Safety contact is normally expected within 3 to 5 days and that the full review may take up to 4 to 6 weeks. The last approved consent screen remains in use during review.
