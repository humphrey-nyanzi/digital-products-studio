One official record

Move from scattered updates to one official competition record

When fixtures, result messages and standings live in separate places, corrections take longer and it becomes harder to confirm what is official. This system gives you one structured path from schedule creation to approved results and current reports.

How it works

From setup to official standings

You remain in control at every step. A submitted result does not affect the official record until you approve it.

  1. Add the competition, teams and venues. Start with the details that shape the schedule and reports.
  2. Create the fixture list. Generate a round-robin schedule or enter fixtures manually.
  3. Collect structured results. Publish eligible fixtures to the connected Google Form.
  4. Approve the official outcome. Review every submission before it changes the record.
  5. Check before publishing. Confirm standings, reports and validation messages are ready.
