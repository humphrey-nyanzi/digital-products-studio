Football Competition Results & Standings Manager

This Google Sheets and Google Forms product helps small football competition organisers create fixtures, collect match results, approve official outcomes, calculate standings and prepare reports in one connected workflow.

Read the User Manual Contact support

Purpose

A clear path from schedule to official report

  1. Set up the competition. Add teams, venues and the basic competition settings.
  2. Create the fixtures. Generate a round-robin schedule or enter fixtures manually.
  3. Collect results. Use the connected Result Form for authorised submissions.
  4. Approve official outcomes. Review each submission before it affects the competition record.
  5. Publish with confidence. Check standings, reports and validation messages before sharing.

Google access

Limited to the product workflow

The product works inside the competition workbook and its connected Google Form. It does not request full Google Drive access or account-wide Google Sheets access.

Competition information remains in the Google files owned by the customer. Read the privacy policy for the exact access, storage and deletion details.
